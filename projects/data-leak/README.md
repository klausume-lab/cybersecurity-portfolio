# Data Leak — Least Privilege Review

**Context:** Google Cybersecurity Certificate coursework / simulation.  
**Status:** Worksheet answers present; supplied versus learner-authored portions not independently verified.

## Scenario

A fictional sales team retained access to an internal folder after a meeting. A representative accidentally shared the internal folder instead of approved promotional materials, and a business partner posted the link publicly.

## Analysis recorded in the worksheet

The identified control is least privilege. The source recommends role-based access to sensitive resources and regular reviews of user privileges. It explains that employee-only links and access reviews would reduce exposure of internal files.

## Interpretation and limits

The course resource connects the exercise to NIST SP 800-53 AC-6. This is a learning reference, not a control assessment or proof of implementation. Publishing an incorrect link is one part of the scenario; the permissions that made the link accessible are the access-control issue. No real data leak or account change was investigated. Original answers and course-provided text may be mixed in the worksheet.
