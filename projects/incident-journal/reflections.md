# Learning reflections

1. Were any activities challenging? Why?
Learning how to read logs and understand command-line tools was challenging because I am still building my technical skills. I had to slow down, pay attention to the commands, and understand what the results were showing instead of guessing. Working through the exercises helped me become more comfortable with the process.


2. Has your understanding of incident detection and response changed?
Yes. I now understand that responding to a security incident involves more than detecting a suspicious event. An analyst must investigate the evidence, decide how serious the threat is, document findings, and follow the correct response process. I also understand why proper communication and post-incident reviews matter.


3. Which tool or concept did you enjoy most? Why?
VirusTotal stood out to me because I could take a suspicious file hash and see how different security vendors classified the file. It connected what I was learning in class to a practical investigation. I want to keep learning how to combine threat intelligence with logs and network analysis.
