# Bank — Risk Register Study

**Context:** Google Cybersecurity Certificate coursework / simulation.  
**Status:** Five scored rows present; ownership of supplied versus learner-entered values unverified.

## Scenario

The course describes a coastal bank with on-site and remote staff, customer accounts, and business partners. This table transcribes the risk entries present in the Drive worksheet; it does not claim every row was independently authored.

| Risk | Vulnerability or event described | Likelihood | Severity | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Business email compromise | Employee tricked into sharing confidential information | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| User database compromise | Poorly encrypted customer data | 2 | 3 | 6 |
| Financial-record exposure | Publicly accessible backup database | 3 | 3 | 9 |
| Theft | Unlocked safe | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| Supply disruption | Delivery delays from natural disasters | 1 | 2 | 2 |

## Review

All priority calculations match likelihood × severity. The values are scenario estimates, not observed incident rates. The highest recorded priority is the publicly accessible backup database. The source notes business-partner exposure and local crime conditions. Coastal weather exposure and other assumptions would need reassessment before applying these scores to a real bank.
