# Klaus Ume | Cybersecurity Portfolio

I am working toward a career in cybersecurity because I want to help organizations keep people and their information safe as AI changes how we use technology. I pay close attention to details, document my work clearly, and stay calm while finding solutions to problems. I value honesty, taking responsibility for my actions, and respecting people’s privacy, and I am building the skills to put those values into practice.

## Focus

My interests include governance, risk, and compliance (GRC), incident response, and protecting sensitive information. This portfolio brings together my available Google Cybersecurity Certificate coursework, explanations, and learning reflections. It is a living collection that can grow with independent labs and original projects.

## How to read this portfolio

These are educational simulations, study artifacts, and AI-assisted portfolio adaptations. Fictional organizations are course scenarios. Course examples and answer keys are references, not my independent investigations. Recommendations are proposals unless an artifact explicitly records a test. No certificate completion, professional affiliation, production deployment, or quantified security improvement is claimed.

Original documents remain in Google Drive. Public versions remove unused template fields, private Drive identifiers, and unnecessary contact details while retaining material limitations. Source documents are not changed by this publication.
